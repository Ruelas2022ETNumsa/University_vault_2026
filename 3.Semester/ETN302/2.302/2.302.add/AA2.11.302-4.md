---
>- Las funciones rampa serán:
>$$u_{(t-1)}:\left\{
\begin{array}{lccl}
3 & \quad t\geq 1 \\
0 & \quad t<1
\end{array}\right.
\qquad\cup\qquad\mbox{-}\,2·r_{(t+1)}:\left\{
\begin{array}{lccl}
-2·(t+1) & \quad t\geq -1 \\
0 & \quad t<-1
\end{array}\right.
\qquad\cup\qquad -1$$

>- Los puntos de interes:
$$f_{(-1)}=(0)+-2·(-1+1)-1\Rightarrow\quad f_{(-1)}=-1$$
$$f_{(1)}=(3)-2·(1+1)-1\Rightarrow\quad f_{(1)}=-2$$

>- Por partes será.
>$$f_{(t)}:\left \{
\begin{array}{lccl}
\quad-1 & &\quad t < -1 \\\\
\quad\mbox{-}\,1\mbox{-}\,2(t+1):\quad \mbox{-}\,2t\,\mbox{-}\,3 & &\quad -1\leq t < 1 \\\\
\quad\mbox{-}\,1\mbox{-}\,2(t+1)+3:\quad \mbox{-}\,2t & &\quad  t \geq 1
\end{array}\right.$$
