>- Para la recta.
>$$(y-y_0)=m·(x-x_{0)\quad}; \quad m=\tan{\alpha}$$
>
>$$(y-e^2)={\color{Orange}\cancelto{{\color{orange}-1}}{{\color{Gray}\tan{45^\circ}}}}·(x-2)$$
>Para los puntos de interseccion con los ejes.
>
>$$\left \{\begin{array}{lccl}
\,y=0 & \rightarrow\quad x=2+e^2 \\
\,x=0 & \rightarrow\quad y=2+e^2
\end{array}\right.$$
>
>Donde:
>$$v_{(t)}=a-t$$
 $$a=2+e^2$$
 
>-  Reemplazando en la ecuacion del valor medio:
>$$V_m=\dfrac{1}{(e^{2}+2)}·\left[\,\int^{2}_{0}e^t\,dt+\int^{e^2+2}_{2}(e^2+2-t)\,dt\right]$$
>
>$$V_m=\dfrac{1}{(e^{2}+1)}·\left\{\,[e^2\mbox{-}1]+\left[\,
{\color{Orange}\cancelto{{\color{orange}e^4+2e^2}}{{\color{Gray}(e^2+2)·(e^2+2\mbox{-}\,2)}}}
\mbox{-}\,\dfrac{{\color{Orange}\cancelto{{\color{orange}e^4+4e^2}}{{\color{Gray} (e^2+2)^2\mbox{-}2^2}}}}{2}
\,\right]\right\}$$
>
>$$V_m=\dfrac{1}{(e^{2}+2)}·\left\{ e^2-1+e^4+2e^2-\dfrac{e^4}{2}-2e^2 \right\}$$
>
>$$V_m=\dfrac{1}{(e^{2}+2)}·\left\{ e^2-1
{\color{Orange}\cancelto{{\color{orange}0.5·e^4}}{{\color{Gray}+e^4}}}
{\color{orange}\cancel{{\color{Gray}+2e^2}}}
{\color{orange}\cancel{{\color{Gray}-\dfrac{e^4}{2}}}}
{\color{orange}\cancel{{\color{Gray}-2e^2}}}
\right\}$$
>
>$$V_m=\dfrac{2e^2-2+e^4}{2(e^2+2)}$$

>- Finalemente:
>**$$V_m=3.588$$**
