## Enunciado.
Graficar la señal.$\qquad i_{(t)}=2·cos(\,2000·t+45^\circ\,)\,\color{Gray}{[\,A\,]}$

---

>- Periodo: $$T=\dfrac{2·\pi}{2000}\quad\Rightarrow\quad T=\pi ·10^{-3}$$
>$$T=3,14·10^{-3}\,\color{Gray}{[seg]}$$

>- Desface:$$45^\circ \mbox{ x } \dfrac{\pi}{180^\circ}= \dfrac{\pi}{4} $$

>- Puntos de interseccion:$$\begin{matrix} 
2000·t+\dfrac{\pi}{4}=0 & \Rightarrow & t=-\dfrac{\pi}{8}·10^{-3} & \Rightarrow &t=-0.39·10^{-3}\,\color{Gray}{[seg]}\\
2000·t+\dfrac{\pi}{4}=\dfrac{\pi}{2} & \Rightarrow & t=\dfrac{\pi}{8}·10^{-3} & \Rightarrow &t=0.39·10^{-3}\,\color{Gray}{[seg]}\\
2000·t+\dfrac{\pi}{4}=\pi & \Rightarrow & t=-\dfrac{3\pi}{8}·10^{-3} & \Rightarrow &t=1.18·10^{-3}\,\color{Gray}{[seg]}\\
2000·t+\dfrac{\pi}{4}=\dfrac{\pi}{4} & \Rightarrow & t=0
\end{matrix}$$
