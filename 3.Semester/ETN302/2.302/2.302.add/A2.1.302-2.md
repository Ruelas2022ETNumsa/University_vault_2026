## Enunciado.
Graficar la señal.$\quad F_{(t)}=\sin(t)·u_{(t-\frac{\pi}{2})}$

---

# Solucion

---

$$F_{(t)}:\left \{
\begin{array}{lccl}
0 &\quad t < \dfrac{\pi}{2}\\
(1)·\sin(t) &\quad t \geq\dfrac{\pi}{2}
\end{array}
\right.$$
