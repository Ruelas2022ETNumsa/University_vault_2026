## Enunciado.
Graficar la señal $\qquad v_{(t)} = u_{(t-3)} + r_{t-2}+sin(t)$

---

# Solucion

---

```ad-Recordando_1
title: F. Rampa...
collapse: closed
![[T2.21.302-2#r_ t]]
```

>- F. paso unitario
>$$u_{(t-3)} =\left \{
\begin{array}{lccl}
\,1 &\quad t \geq 3 \\
\,0 &\quad t > 1
\end{array}
\right.$$

>- F. rampa
>$$r_{(t-2)} =\left \{
\begin{array}{lccl}
\,1 &\quad t \geq 3 \\
\,0 &\quad t > 1
\end{array}
\right.$$

>- Por partes como funcion.
>$$v_{(t)} =\left \{
\begin{array}{lccl}
\,\sin(t) & \quad t \leq 2 \\\\
\,\sin(t)+r_{(t-2)} & \quad 2\leq t<3 \\\\
\,\sin(t)+r_{(t-2)}+u_{t-3} & \quad 3 \leq t
\end{array}
\right.$$
