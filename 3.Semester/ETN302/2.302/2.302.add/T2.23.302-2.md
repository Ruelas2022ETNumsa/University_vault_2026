## $$e^{-\alpha·t}$$

---

$$f_{(t)}=A·e^{-\alpha ·t}\quad
\begin{cases}
t=0 & \Rightarrow\quad f=A\\
t\rightarrow\infty & \Rightarrow\quad f\rightarrow 0 \\
t\rightarrow-\infty & \Rightarrow\quad f\rightarrow\infty
\end{cases}$$
