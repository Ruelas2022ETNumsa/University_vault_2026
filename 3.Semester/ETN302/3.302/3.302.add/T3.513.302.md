# Divisores de corriente-Capacitores.

---

### Circuito.
![[TE3.513.302-1.png|450]]

### Deducción.
- Ley de corrientes de kirchhoff (L.C.K).
$$I_{T}  =  I_{a}+I_{b}+I_{c} \qquad\dots (1)$$

>- Por Ohm.
>$$V=I·X_{C}$$
>Donde.
>$$X_{C}=\frac{1}{\omega ·C}$$
>$$X_{C}\equiv \mbox{Reactancia capacitiva}\,[\,\Omega\,]$$
>- $$\Rightarrow\quad V=\frac{I}{\omega ·C}\qquad\circledast$$

- Voltaje en paralelo a y b.
$$\begin{array}{rcl}
V_{a} & = & V_{b}\\
\dfrac{I_{a}}{{\color{orange}\bcancel{{\color{gray}\omega}}}·C_{a}} & = &\dfrac{I_{b}}{{\color{orange}\bcancel{{\color{gray}\omega}}}·C_{b}}\\
\end{array}$$
$$\looparrowright I_{b}=\dfrac{C_{b}}{C_{a}}·I_{a} \qquad\dots (2)$$

- Voltaje en paralelo a y c.
$$\begin{array}{rcl}
V_{a} & = & V_{c}\\
\dfrac{I_{a}}{{\color{orange}\bcancel{{\color{gray}\omega}}}·C_{a}} & = &\dfrac{I_{c}}{{\color{orange}\bcancel{{\color{gray}\omega}}}·C_{c}}\\
\end{array}$$
$$\looparrowright I_{c}=\dfrac{C_{c}}{C_{a}}·I_{a} \qquad\dots (3)$$

- Reemplazando ec. (2) y (3) en (1).
$$I_a=\dfrac{C_{a}}{C_{a}+C_{b}+C_{x}}\,·\,I_T$$

- Generalizando.
$$I_n=\dfrac{C_{n}}{C_{a}+C_{b}+C_{c}}\,·\,I_T\quad ; \quad n=a,b,c.$$
