## Resolver el circuito.
Sea el circuito de la figura.
![[TE3.6.302-01.png\|220]]
Si $i_{1}=6·\sin(800·t)$.Hallar la tension sobre el resistor de $10\,[\,\Omega\,]$.

---

# Solucion

---

```ad-Recordando_1
title: Divisores de corriente...
collapse: closed
![[T3.511.302#Resumen]]
```

>- Dibujando las corrientes y el voltaje.
>![[TE3.6.302-1.png\|220]]

>- Dato:
>$$i_{1}=6·\sin(800t)$$

>- Encontramos $i_{\mbox{x}}$ ,por Div. de corriente.
>$${\color{orange}\cancelto{{\color{orange}6·\sin(800·t)}}{{\color{gray}i_{1}}}}=\dfrac{15}{15+5}·i_{\mbox{x}}$$
>
>$$\looparrowright\quad i_{\mbox{x}}=
\dfrac{{\color{orange}\cancelto{{\color{orange}4}}{{\color{gray}20}}}}{{\color{orange}\cancelto{{\color{orange}3}}{{\color{gray}15}}}}·6·\sin(800·t)$$
>
>$$\looparrowright\quad i_{\mbox{x}}=
\dfrac{4}{{\color{orange}\cancel{{\color{gray}3}}}}·{\color{orange}\cancelto{{\color{orange}2}}{{\color{gray}6}}}·\sin(800·t)$$
>
>$$\Rightarrow\quad i_{\mbox{x}}=8·\sin(800·t)$$

>- Por la Ley de Ohm.
>$$v_{\mbox{x}}=10·[\,8·\sin(800·t)\,]$$
>
>$$v_{\mbox{x}}=80·\sin(800·t)\,[\,V\,]$$
