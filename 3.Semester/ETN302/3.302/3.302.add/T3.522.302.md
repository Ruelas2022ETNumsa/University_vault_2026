# Divisores de tension-Inductores.

---

### Circuito.
![[TE3.522.302-1.png|350]]

### Deducción.
- Ley de tensiones de kirchhoff (L.T.K).
$$v_{(t)}  =  v_{a}+v_{b}+v_{c} \qquad\dots (1)$$

>- Por Ohm.
>$$v_{L}=i·X_{L}$$
>Donde.
>$$X_{L}=\omega ·L$$
>$$X_{L}\equiv \mbox{Reactancia inductiva}\,[\,\Omega\,]$$
>- $$\Rightarrow\quad v_{L}=i·\omega·L\qquad\circledast$$




- Reemplazando $\circledast$ en  $(1)$.
$$v_{(t)}=i·\omega L_{a}+i·\omega L_{b}+i·\omega L_{c}$$
$$\looparrowright v_{(t)}=i·\omega ·(L_{a}+L_{b}+L_{c})$$
$$\Rightarrow\quad \dfrac{v_{(t)}}{L_{a}+L_{b}+L_{c}}=i·\omega \qquad\dots (2)$$



- Generalizando.
$${\color{orange}\cancelto{{\color{orange}v_{n}/(\omega·L_{n})}}{{\color{gray}i}}}·\omega=\dfrac{v_{(t)}}{L_{a}+L_{b}+L_{c}}$$
$$\looparrowright \dfrac{v_{n}}{{\color{orange}\bcancel {{\color{gray}\omega}}}·L_{n}}·{\color{orange}\bcancel {{\color{gray}\omega}}}=\dfrac{v_{(t)}}{L_{a}+L_{b}+L_{c}}$$

$$v_{n}=\dfrac{L_{n}}{L_{a}+L_{b}+L_{c}}·v_{(t)}\quad ; \quad n=a,b,c.$$
