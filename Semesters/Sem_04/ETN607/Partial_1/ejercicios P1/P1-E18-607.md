##### Ej. Una masa $m$ orbita alrededor de una masa fija $M$ ubicada en el origen. El sistema opera en el espacio 3D con coordenadas cilíndricas $(r, \theta, z)$. Coordenadas cartesianas: $x = r\cos\theta$, $y = r\sin\theta$, $z = z$. Fuerzas: $F_x = -\frac{GMm}{\rho^2}\cos\theta$, $F_y = -\frac{GMm}{\rho^2}\sin\theta$, $F_z = -\frac{GMmz}{\rho^3}$, donde $\rho = \sqrt{r^2 + z^2}$ es la distancia real al origen. Obtener las GDE para $q_1 = r$, $q_2 = \theta$, $q_3 = z$.

**Resolución**
Aplicación de las ecuaciones de Lagrange mediante la obtención de la energía cinética del cuerpo y la proyección de las fuerzas gravitacionales en cada dirección cilíndrica.


$$
\begin{array}{rcl}
x & = & r\cos\theta \implies \dot{x} = \dot{r}\cos\theta - r\dot\theta\sin\theta \\
y & = & r\sin\theta \implies \dot{y} = \dot{r}\sin\theta + r\dot\theta\cos\theta \\
z & = & z \implies \dot{z} = \dot{z} \\
T & = & \dfrac{1}{2}m\left(\dot{x}^2 + \dot{y}^2 + \dot{z}^2\right) \\
T & = & \dfrac{1}{2}m\left[(\dot{r}\cos\theta - r\dot\theta\sin\theta)^2 + (\dot{r}\sin\theta + r\dot\theta\cos\theta)^2 + \dot{z}^2\right] \\
T & = & \dfrac{1}{2}m\left[\dot{r}^2\cos^2\theta - 2r\dot{r}\dot\theta\sin\theta\cos\theta + r^2\dot\theta^2\sin^2\theta + \dot{r}^2\sin^2\theta + 2r\dot{r}\dot\theta\sin\theta\cos\theta + r^2\dot\theta^2\cos^2\theta + \dot{z}^2\right] \\
T & = & \dfrac{1}{2}m\left[\dot{r}^2(\cos^2\theta + \sin^2\theta) + r^2\dot\theta^2(\sin^2\theta + \cos^2\theta) + \dot{z}^2\right] \\
T & = & \dfrac{1}{2}m\left(\dot{r}^2 + r^2\dot\theta^2 + \dot{z}^2\right)
\end{array}
$$


Para la coordenada $q_1 = r$:

$$
\begin{array}{rcl}
\dfrac{\partial T}{\partial \dot{r}} & = & m\dot{r} \implies \dfrac{d}{dt}\!\left(\dfrac{\partial T}{\partial \dot{r}}\right) = m\ddot{r} \\
\dfrac{\partial T}{\partial r} & = & mr\dot\theta^2 \\
Q_r & = & F_x\dfrac{\partial x}{\partial r} + F_y\dfrac{\partial y}{\partial r} + F_z\dfrac{\partial z}{\partial r} \\
Q_r & = & \left(-\dfrac{GMm}{\rho^2}\cos\theta\right)\cos\theta + \left(-\dfrac{GMm}{\rho^2}\sin\theta\right)\sin\theta + 0 \\
Q_r & = & -\dfrac{GMm}{\rho^2}(\cos^2\theta + \sin^2\theta) = -\dfrac{GMm}{\rho^2} \\[4pt]
& & \text{con } \rho^2 = r^2+z^2 \implies Q_r = -\dfrac{GMm}{r^2+z^2} \\
\dfrac{d}{dt}\!\left(\dfrac{\partial T}{\partial \dot{r}}\right) - \dfrac{\partial T}{\partial r} & = & Q_r \\
m\ddot{r} - mr\dot\theta^2 & = & -\dfrac{GMm}{r^2+z^2} \\
\therefore\quad \color{orange}{m(\ddot{r} - r\dot\theta^2) + \dfrac{GMm}{r^2+z^2}} & \color{orange}{=} & \color{orange}{0}
\end{array}
$$


Para la coordenada $q_2 = \theta$:

$$
\begin{array}{rcl}
\dfrac{\partial T}{\partial \dot\theta} & = & mr^2\dot\theta \implies \dfrac{d}{dt}\!\left(\dfrac{\partial T}{\partial \dot\theta}\right) = m(r^2\ddot\theta + 2r\dot{r}\dot\theta) \\
\dfrac{\partial T}{\partial \theta} & = & 0 \\
Q_\theta & = & F_x\dfrac{\partial x}{\partial \theta} + F_y\dfrac{\partial y}{\partial \theta} + F_z\dfrac{\partial z}{\partial \theta} \\
Q_\theta & = & \left(-\dfrac{GMm}{\rho^2}\cos\theta\right)(-r\sin\theta) + \left(-\dfrac{GMm}{\rho^2}\sin\theta\right)(r\cos\theta) + 0 \\
Q_\theta & = & \dfrac{GMm r}{\rho^2}\sin\theta\cos\theta - \dfrac{GMm r}{\rho^2}\sin\theta\cos\theta = 0 \\
\dfrac{d}{dt}\!\left(\dfrac{\partial T}{\partial \dot\theta}\right) - \dfrac{\partial T}{\partial \theta} & = & Q_\theta \\
\therefore\quad \color{orange}{m(r^2\ddot\theta + 2r\dot{r}\dot\theta)} & \color{orange}{=} & \color{orange}{0}
\end{array}
$$


Para la coordenada $q_3 = z$:

$$
\begin{array}{rcl}
\dfrac{\partial T}{\partial \dot{z}} & = & m\dot{z} \implies \dfrac{d}{dt}\!\left(\dfrac{\partial T}{\partial \dot{z}}\right) = m\ddot{z} \\
\dfrac{\partial T}{\partial z} & = & 0 \\
Q_z & = & F_x\dfrac{\partial x}{\partial z} + F_y\dfrac{\partial y}{\partial z} + F_z\dfrac{\partial z}{\partial z} \\
Q_z & = & 0 + 0 + \left(-\dfrac{GMmz}{\rho^3}\right)(1) \\
Q_z & = & -\dfrac{GMmz}{(r^2+z^2)^{3/2}} \\
\dfrac{d}{dt}\!\left(\dfrac{\partial T}{\partial \dot{z}}\right) - \dfrac{\partial T}{\partial z} & = & Q_z \\
m\ddot{z} & = & -\dfrac{GMmz}{(r^2+z^2)^{3/2}} \\
\therefore\quad \color{orange}{m\ddot{z} + \dfrac{GMmz}{(r^2+z^2)^{3/2}}} & \color{orange}{=} & \color{orange}{0}
\end{array}
$$
