# Solucion.
>- De la grafica.
>	- Para la recta $L_1$.
>$$\dfrac{x-3}{2-3}=\dfrac{y-0}{10-0}\Rightarrow\quad i=30-10t$$
>	- Para la recta $L_2$.
>$$\dfrac{x-6}{6-5}=\dfrac{y-0}{0\,\mbox{-}\,(\,\mbox{-}\,10)}\Rightarrow\quad i=10t-60$$

>- Sea la corriente $i_{(t)}$.
>$$i_{(t)}[\,A\,] =\left \{
\begin{array}{lccl}
\quad 10·t & &    0\leq t<1  \\\\
\quad 10 & &    1\leq t<2  \\\\
\quad 30\,\mbox{-}\,10·t & &    2\leq t<4  \\\\
\quad \mbox{-}\,10 & &    4\leq t<5  \\\\
\quad 10·t\,\mbox{-}\,60 & &    5\leq t<6
\end{array}
\qquad\right.
\left \|
\begin{array}{lccl}\\\\
\quad \tfrac{di}{dt} \\
\quad \Longrightarrow \\\\\\\\
\end{array}
\right.
\qquad \dfrac{di}{dt}=\,\left \{
\begin{array}{lccl}
\quad 10 &  & 0\leq t<1  \\\\
\quad 0 &  & 1\leq t<2  \\\\
\quad \mbox{-}\,10 &  & 2\leq t<4  \\\\
\quad 0 &  & 4\leq t<5  \\\\
\quad 10 &  & 5\leq t<6
\end{array}
\right.$$

- Finalmente el voltaje será.
>$$V_{L(t)} =L·\dfrac{di}{dt}:\,\left \{
\begin{array}{lccl}
10·2m\rightarrow & 20\,[mV] & 0\leq t<1  \\\\
& 0 & 1\leq t<2  \\\\
\mbox{-}\,10·2m\rightarrow & \mbox{-}\,20m\,[mV] & 2\leq t<4  \\\\
& 0 & 4\leq t<5  \\\\
10·2m\rightarrow & 20m\,[mV] & 5\leq t<6
\end{array}
\right.$$
