## Resolver el circuito.
Sea el circuito de la figura.
![[TE3.6.302-2.png\|250]]

Calcular el valor de $R_{x}$, para que I sea igual a $55\,[\,mA\,]$.
Donde:
$$V=100[\,V\,]$$
$$R=1[\,k\,\Omega\,]$$

# Solucion

>- Por la Ley de Ohm.
>$$V=I·\left(R+2R//(R_{\mbox{x}}+R)\right)$$
>
>(Resistencia equivalente.)
>$$V=I·\left(R+\dfrac{2R·(R_{\mbox{x}}+R)}{2R+R_{\mbox{x}}+R}\right)$$
>
>(Depejando y factorizando.)
>$$\dfrac{V}{IR}=\dfrac{3R+R_{\mbox{x}}+2R_{\mbox{x}}+2R}{3R+R_{\mbox{x}}}=\dfrac{5R+3R_{\mbox{x}}}{3R+R_{\mbox{x}}}$$
>
>(Simplificando queda.)
>$$\dfrac{V}{IR}·(3R+R_{\mbox{x}})=5R+3R{\mbox{x}}$$
>
>(Factorizando $R_{\mbox{x}}$.)
>$$R_{\mbox{x}}·\left(\dfrac{V}{IR}-3\right)=5R-3{\color{orange}\bcancel {{\color{gray}R}}}·\dfrac{V}{I·{\color{orange}\bcancel {{\color{gray}R}}}}$$
>
>(Despejando $R_{\mbox{x}}$.)
>$$R_{\mbox{x}}=\dfrac{\frac{5RI-3V}{{\color{orange}\bcancel {{\color{gray}I}}}}}
{\frac{V-3IR}{{\color{orange}\bcancel {{\color{gray}I}}}·R}}=\dfrac{R·(5IR-3V)}{V-3IR}$$
>
>(Reemplzando los valores.)
>$$R_{\mbox{x}}=\dfrac{10^{3}·(5·55{\color{orange}\bcancel {{\color{gray}m}}}{\color{orange}\bcancel {{\color{gray}k}}}-3·100)}{100-3·55{\color{orange}\bcancel {{\color{gray}m}}}{\color{orange}\bcancel {{\color{gray}k}}}}=\dfrac{10^{3}(275-300)}{100-165}$$

>- Finalmente el valor de $R_{\mbox{x}}$ es.
>$$R_{\mbox{x}}=384.615\,[\,\Omega\,]$$
