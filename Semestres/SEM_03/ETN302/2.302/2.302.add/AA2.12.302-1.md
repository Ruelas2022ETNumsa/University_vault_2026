# Solucion

---

>- Para los periodos.
>$${\color{orange}\cancelto{{\color{orange}\pi}}{{\color{gray}\omega}}}=\dfrac{2\pi}{T}\Rightarrow\quad T_{(1,2)}=2$$

>- Simplificando.
>$$f_{(t)}=2·[\,(1\,\mbox{-}\,t)·u_{(1-t)}·\sin(t\pi)\,]-
[\,(t\,\mbox{-}\,1)·u_{(t-1)}·\cos(t\pi)\,]$$
>
>$$f_{(t)}=2·[\,(1\,\mbox{-}\,t)·u_{(1-t)}·\sin(t\pi)\,]+
[\,(1\,\mbox{-}\,t)·u_{(t-1)}·\cos(t\pi)\,]$$
>
>$$f_{(t)}=(1\,\mbox{-}\,t)·
>\{\,2·u_{(1-t)}·\sin(t\pi)+
u_{(t-1)}·\cos(t\pi)\,\}$$

>- Por tramos.
>$$\left[ \,1-t\,\right]
\quad\cap\quad
\left[\, g_{1}:\left\{
\begin{array}{lccl}
2\sin(t\pi) & t\leq 1 \\
0 & ; t>1
\end{array}\right.
\quad\cup\quad
g_{2}:\left\{
\begin{array}{lccl}
\cos(t\pi) & t\geq 1 \\
0 & ; t<1
\end{array}\right.
\right]$$

>- Los punto de intere:
>$$f_{(1)}=(1-1)[\,
{\color{orange}\cancelto{{\color{orange}0}}{{\color{gray}2\sin(\pi)}}}
+{\color{orange}\cancelto{{\color{orange}1}}{{\color{gray}\cos(\pi)}}}
\,]\Rightarrow\quad f_{(1)}=0$$





>- Por partes será.
>$$f_{(t)}:\left \{
\begin{array}{lccl}
\quad(1-t)·2\sin(t\pi) &\quad t < 1 \\\\
\quad 0 & \quad t=1\\\\
\quad (1-t)·\cos(t\pi): & \quad t > 1
\end{array}\right.$$
