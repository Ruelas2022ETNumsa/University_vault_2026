## Enunciado.
Sabiendo que el valor eficaz del voltaje $\;V_{(t)}:\quad V_t=100+v_m·\sin(500·t)$ es 103 [V]. Hallar el valor máximo "$V_{máx}$".

---

# Solucion

---

```ad-Recordando_1
title: Parámetros basicos y rectas...
collapse: closed
![[T2.22.302-2#Parámetros básicos]]
```

>- Sabiendo que:
>$$\omega=500\qquad \Rightarrow \quad T=\dfrac{2\pi}{500} \quad \Rightarrow \quad T=\dfrac{\pi}{250}$$

>- Reemplazando en la ecuacion:
>$${\color{Orange}\cancelto{{\color{orange}103}}{{\color{Gray}V_{ef}}}}
=\sqrt{\dfrac{250}{\pi}·\int^{T}_{0}\left( 100+V_m\sin(\omega t) \right)^{2\,}dt}$$
>
>$$(103^{2})·\dfrac{\pi}{250}=\int^{T}_{0} \left[ 10^4+200·V_m·\sin(\omega·t)+V^2_m· {\color{Orange}\cancelto{{\color{orange}\dfrac{1-\cos(2\omega·t)}{2}}}{{\color{Gray}\sin^2(\omega·t)}}}\right]dt$$
>
>$$\dfrac{103^2·\pi}{250}=
\int^{T}_{0}\left[ 10^4+200·V_m·\sin(\omega·t)+\dfrac{V^2_m}{2}-\dfrac{V^2_m·\cos(2\omega·t)}{2} \right]dt$$
>
>$$\dfrac{130^2·\pi}{250}=
\left(10^4 + \dfrac{V^2_m}{2}\right) \left[ \,t\, \right]\bigg |^T_0
-\dfrac{200·V_m}{\omega}·\cos(\omega·t)\bigg |^T_0
-\dfrac{V^2_m}{4·\omega}·\sin(2·\omega·t)\bigg|^T_0$$
>
>$$103^2T=\left(10^4 + \dfrac{V^2_m}{2}\right)(T\mbox{-}\,0)\,
\mbox{-}\,\dfrac{200·V_m}{\omega}[\,\cos({\color{Orange}\cancelto{{\color{orange} 2\pi }}{{\color{Gray} \omega·T }}})\,\mbox{-}\,1\,]\,
\mbox{-}\,\dfrac{V^2_m}{4\omega}[\,\sin(2{\color{Orange}\cancelto{{\color{orange} 2\pi }}{{\color{Gray} \omega·T }}})\,\mbox{-}\,0\,]$$
>
>$$(103^2-10^4)·{\color{orange}\cancel{{\color{Gray} T }}}=\dfrac{V^2_m·{\color{orange}\cancel{{\color{Gray} T }}}}{2}
{\color{orange}\cancel{{\color{Gray} -\dfrac{200·V_m}{\omega}·[\,1-1\,] }}}
{\color{orange}\cancel{{\color{Gray} -\dfrac{v^2_m}{4·\omega}·[\,0\,] }}}$$
>
>$$\sqrt{2·(103^2-10^4)}=V_m$$

>- Finalmente:
>$$V_{máx}=34.899\,[\,V\,]$$
