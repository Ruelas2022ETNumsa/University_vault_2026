# Divisores de tension-Capacitores.

---

### Circuito.
![[TE3.523.302-1.png\|350]]

### Deducción.
- Ley de tensiones de kirchhoff (L.T.K).
$$v_{(t)}  =  v_{(a)}+v_{(b)}+v_{(c)} \qquad\dots (1)$$

>- Por Ohm.
>$$v=i·X_{C}$$
>Donde.
>$$X_{C}=\frac{1}{\omega ·C}$$
>$$X_{C}\equiv \mbox{Reactancia capacitiva}\,[\,\Omega\,]$$
>- $$\Rightarrow\quad v=\frac{i}{\omega ·C}\qquad\circledast$$

- Reemplazando $\circledast$ en $(1)$.
$$v_{(t)}=\frac{i}{\omega ·C_{a}}+\frac{i}{\omega ·C_{b}}+\frac{i}{\omega ·C_{c}}$$


$$\looparrowright v_{(t)} = \dfrac{i}{\omega}\left( \dfrac{1}{C_{a}}+\dfrac{1}{C_{b}}+\dfrac{1}{C_{c}} \right)$$
$$\looparrowright v_{(t)} = \dfrac{{\color{orange}\cancelto{{\color{orange}v_{a}·\omega·C_{a}}}{{\color{gray}i}}}}{\omega}\left( \dfrac{C_{b}·C_{c}+C_{a}·C_{c}+C_{a}·C_{b}}{C_{a}·C_{b}·C_{c}} \right)$$
$$\looparrowright v_{(t)} = \dfrac{
v_{a}·{\color{orange}\bcancel {{\color{gray}\omega}}}·{\color{orange}\bcancel {{\color{gray}C_{a}}}}
}{{\color{orange}\bcancel {{\color{gray}\omega}}}}\left( \dfrac{C_{b}·C_{c}+C_{a}·C_{c}+C_{a}·C_{b}}{{\color{orange}\bcancel {{\color{gray}C_{a}}}}·C_{b}·C_{c}} \right)$$
- Finalmente.

$$v_{a}=\dfrac{C_{b}·C_{c}}{C_{a}·C_{b}+C_{a}·C_{c}+C_{b}·C_{c}}\,·\,v_{(t)}\quad\circledast$$
