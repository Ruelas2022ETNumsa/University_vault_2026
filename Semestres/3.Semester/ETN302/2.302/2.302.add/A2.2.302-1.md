# Solucion

---

>- Identificando valores de la señal.
>$$f_{(t)}=3·\sin\left[
\underset{{\color{orange} \omega }}{\underline{{\color{Gray} 30 }}}
·\left(t+
\underset{{\color{orange} t_0 }}{\underline{{\color{Gray} \dfrac{70^\circ}{30} }}}
\right) \right]$$

>- Para el periodo.
>$${\color{orange}\cancelto{{\color{orange} 30 }}{{\color{Gray} 
 \omega }}}=\dfrac{2·\pi}{T} \Rightarrow\quad T=\dfrac{\pi}{15}$$

>- Para el desfase
>$$t_0=\dfrac{70^\circ}{30}\times\dfrac{\pi}{180^\circ}\Rightarrow
\quad t_0=\dfrac{7\pi}{54}$$

>- Para el valor medio.
>$$V_m=\dfrac{15}{\pi}·\int^{T}_{0}3·\sin\left[30·\left( t+\dfrac{\pi}{27·2} \right) \right]dt$$
>
>$$V_m=\dfrac{45}{\pi}·\left(\dfrac{1}{30}  \right)·
\left\{ -\cos\left[ 30·\left( t+\dfrac{7\pi}{54} \right) \right] \right\}\Bigg |_0^{\pi/15}$$
>
>$$V_m=\dfrac{\mbox{-}\,3}{2\pi}\left\{ \cos\left[30·\left( 
\dfrac{\pi}{15}+\dfrac{7\pi}{54}
\right) \right]
-\cos\left[ 30·\left( \dfrac{7\pi}{54} \right) \right] 
\right\}$$
>
>$$V_m=\dfrac{\mbox{-}\,3}{2\pi}·{\color{orange}\cancelto{{\color{orange} 0 }}{{\color{Gray} (0.94-0.94) }}}\Rightarrow\quad V_m=0$$

>- Tomando solo medio periodo.
>$$V_m=\dfrac{30}{\pi}·\int^{\pi/30}_{0}3·\sin(30t+70^\circ)dt=
\dfrac{90}{\pi}·\left[ \dfrac{\mbox{-}\,\cos(30t+70^\circ)}{30} \right]\Bigg |^{\pi/30}_{0}$$
>
>$$V_m=\dfrac{\mbox{-}\,3}{\pi}·({\color{Orange}\cancelto{{\color{Orange} \mbox{-}\,\cos(70^\circ) }}{{\color{Gray} \cos(\pi+70^\circ) }}}-\cos(70^\circ))=\dfrac{3·2·\cos(70^\circ)}{\pi}$$
>
>$$V_m=0.653$$

>- Para el factor de forma.
>$$V_{\varepsilon}=\sqrt{\dfrac{30}{\pi}·\int^{\pi/30}_{0}3^2·\sin^2(30t+70^\circ)dt}$$
>
>$$V_{\varepsilon}=3·\sqrt{
\dfrac{30}{\pi}·
\int^{\pi/30}_{0}\dfrac{1-\cos(2·(30t+70^\circ))}{2}
dt}$$
>
>Sea la integral:
>$$I= \int^{\pi/30}_{0}\dfrac{1-\cos(2·(30t+70^\circ))}{2}
dt$$
>
>$$I=\dfrac{1}{2}·t\Bigg |^{\pi/30}_{0}
-\dfrac{\sin(2·(30t+70^\circ))}{120}\Bigg |^{\pi/30}_0$$
>
>$$I=\dfrac{\pi}{60}-0-\dfrac{1}{120}·\left[ 
{\color{Orange}\cancelto{{\color{Orange} \sin(140^\circ) }}{{\color{Gray} \sin(2\pi+140^\circ) }}}
-\sin(140^\circ)
\right]$$
>
>$$I=\dfrac{\pi}{60}-0\Rightarrow\quad I=\dfrac{\pi}{60}$$
>
>En la ecuacion.
>$$V_\varepsilon = 3·\sqrt{\dfrac{30}{\pi}\times\dfrac{\pi}{60}}\Rightarrow\quad V_\varepsilon =\dfrac{3\sqrt{2}}{2}$$

>- Finalemente para el factor de forma.
>$$f.f.=\dfrac{3\sqrt{2}/2}{0.653}$$
>$$f.f.=3.248$$
