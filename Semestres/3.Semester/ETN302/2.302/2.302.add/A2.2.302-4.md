# Solucion

---

>- Sea la señal.
>$$f_{(t)}-(\mbox{-}\,2)=\sin(\pi t-90^\circ)·[\,t-0\,]^2$$
>![[AD2.2.302-3.svg]]

>- Para el periodo: $\qquad\sin\left[ \underset{{\color{Orange} \omega }}{\underline{{\color{Gray} \pi }}} · \left( t-\underset{{\color{Orange} t_0 }}{\underline{{\color{Gray} \dfrac{90^{\circ}}{\pi} }}} \right)\right]$
>
>$$\hookrightarrow\quad\omega =\dfrac{2\pi}{T}\Rightarrow \quad T=2$$

>- Para el valor medio.
>$$V_{m}=\dfrac{1}{2}·\int^{2}_{0}t^2·
{\color{orange}\cancelto{{\color{Orange} -\cos(\pi t) }}{{\color{Gray} \sin(\pi t-90^{\circ}) }}}-2\,dt=
\dfrac{\mbox{-}\,1}{2}·\int^{2}_{0}t^2·\cos(\pi t)+2dt$$
>
>Operando la 1 seccion de la integral.
>$$I_1=\int^{2}_{0}t^2·\cos(\pi t)dt$$

>O.A.
>$$\begin{matrix}
\mathbf{{}_{d/dt}} && \mathbf{{}_{\int dt}}&&\mathbf{I_1}\\\\
t^2 & | & \cos(t\pi)\\
2·t & | & \frac{\sin(t\pi)}{\pi} & \Rightarrow & t^2·\frac{\sin(t\pi)}{\pi}\\
2 & | & \frac{1}{\pi}·\left( \frac{-\cos(t\pi)}{\pi} \right) & \Rightarrow & {\color{orange}(-1)}·2t·\frac{\mbox{-}\cos(t\pi)}{\pi^2} \\
0 & | & \frac{1}{\pi^2}·\left( \frac{-\sin(t\pi)}{\pi} \right) & \Rightarrow & 2·\frac{\mbox{-}\sin(t\pi)}{\pi^3}\\
\vdots& | & \vdots & \Rightarrow & {{\color{orange}(-1)}·0\dots}
\end{matrix}$$

>$$I_1=\frac{t^2·\sin(t\pi)}{\pi}+
\frac{\mbox{+}2t·\cos(t\pi)}{\pi^2}+
\frac{\mbox{-}2·\sin(t\pi)}{\pi^3}$$
>
>Para la 2da seccion de la integral.
>$$I_2=\int 2dt=2t$$
>
>En la ecuacion.
>$$V_m=\dfrac{\mbox{-}1}{2}·\left( 
\frac{t^2·\sin(t\pi)}{\pi}+
\frac{2t·\cos(t\pi)}{\pi^2}-
\frac{2·\sin(t\pi)}{\pi^3}+2t
\right)\Bigg |^{2}_{0}$$
>
>$$V_m=\dfrac{\mbox{-}1}{2}\left\{ 
\left[ 
\dfrac{1}{\pi}(2^2{\color{orange}\cancelto{{\color{Orange}0}}{{\color{Gray}\sin(2\pi)}}}\,\mbox{-}\,0)+
\dfrac{2}{\pi^2}(2{\color{orange}\cancelto{{\color{Orange} 1 }}{{\color{Gray} \cos(2\pi) }}}\,\mbox{-}\,0·1)\,\mbox{-}\,
\dfrac{2}{\pi^3}({\color{orange}\cancelto{{\color{Orange}0}}{{\color{Gray}\sin(2\pi)}}}\,\mbox{-}\,0)
\right]+2(2\,\mbox{-}\,0)\right\}$$
>
>$$V_m=\dfrac{\mbox{-}1}{2}·\left( 
\dfrac{2·2}{\pi^2}+4\right)\Rightarrow\quad
V_m=\dfrac{\mbox{-}2}{\pi^2}-2$$
>
>$$V_m=-2.203$$

>- Para el valor eficaz.
>$$V_{\varepsilon}=\sqrt{\dfrac{1}{2}·\int^{2}_{0}[t^2·\cos(t\pi)-2]^2dt}$$
>
>Para la integral.
>$$I_{3}=\int t^4·\cos^2(t\pi)-4·t^2·\cos(t\pi)+4\;dt$$

>O.A.
>$$C^2+S^2=1-C2+{\color{orange}\cancelto{{\color{Orange} C^2-S^2 }}{{\color{Gray} C2 }}}\Rightarrow\quad C^2=\frac{1}{2}·(1+C2)$$

>En la integral.
>$$I_{3}=\int t^4·\frac{1}{2}·[1+\cos(2t\pi)]-4·t^2·\cos(t\pi)+4\;dt$$

>O.A.
>- $\qquad I^{`}_{3}=\int t^4·\cos(2t\pi)dt\quad:$
>$$\begin{matrix}
\mathbf{{}_{d/dt}} && \mathbf{{}_{\int dt}}&&\mathbf{I_3^{`}}\\\\
t^4 & | & \cos(2t\pi)\\
4t^3 & | & \frac{\sin(2t\pi)}{2\pi} & \Rightarrow & t^4·\frac{\sin(2t\pi)}{2\pi}\\
12t^2 & | & \frac{1}{2\pi}·\left( \frac{\mbox{-}\cos(2t\pi)}{2\pi} \right) & \Rightarrow & 
{\color{orange}(-1)}·(4t^3)·\frac{1}{2\pi}\left( \frac{\mbox{-}\cos(2t\pi)}{2\pi} \right)\\
24t & | & \frac{\mbox{-}1}{(2\pi)^2}·\left( \frac{\sin(2t\pi)}{2\pi} \right) & \Rightarrow &
12t^2·\frac{\mbox{-}1}{(2\pi)^2}\left( \frac{\sin(2t\pi)}{2\pi} \right)\\
24 & | & \frac{\mbox{-}1}{(2\pi)^3}·\left( \frac{\mbox{-}\cos(2t\pi)}{2\pi} \right) & \Rightarrow &
{\color{orange}(-1)}·24t·\frac{\mbox{-}1}{(2\pi)^3}·\left( \frac{\mbox{-}\cos(2t\pi)}{2\pi} \right)\\
0 & | & \frac{\mbox{+}1}{(2\pi)^4}·\left( \frac{\sin(2t\pi)}{2\pi} \right) & \Rightarrow &
24·\frac{\mbox{+}1}{(2\pi)^4}·\left( \frac{\sin(2t\pi)}{2\pi} \right)\\
\vdots& | & \vdots & \Rightarrow & {{\color{orange}(-1)}·0\dots}
\end{matrix}$$

>- Evaluando en sus limites.
>$$I^{`}_{3}=\left[
\frac{t^4·\sin(2t\pi)}{2\pi}+
\frac{t^{3}·\cos(2t\pi)}{\pi^{2}}-
\frac{3t^{2}\sin(2t\pi)}{2\pi^{3}}-
\frac{3t\cos(2t\pi)}{2\pi^{4}}+
\frac{3\sin(2t\pi)}{4\pi^{5}}\quad
\right]\Bigg |^{2}_{0}$$
>
>$$I^{`}_{3}=\left( \frac{t^4}{2\pi} \,\mbox{-}\,\frac{3t^2}{2\pi^{3}}+\frac{3}{4\pi^{5}}\right)·{\color{orange}\cancelto{{\color{Orange} 0 }}{{\color{Gray} \sin(2t\pi) }}}
+\frac{1}{\pi^{2}}(2^3·{\color{orange}\cancelto{{\color{Orange} 1 }}{{\color{Gray} \cos(4\pi) }}}\,\mbox{-}\,0)
-\frac{3}{2\pi^{4}}(2·{\color{orange}\cancelto{{\color{Orange} 1 }}{{\color{Gray} \cos(4\pi) }}}\,\mbox{-}\,0)$$
>
>$$I^{`}_{3}=\frac{8}{\pi^{2}}-\frac{3}{\pi^{4}}$$

>O.A
>- $\qquad I^{``}_{3}=4t^2·\cos(t\pi) \quad:$
>$$\begin{matrix}
\mathbf{{}_{d/dt}} && \mathbf{{}_{\int dt}}&&\mathbf{I_3^{`}}\\\\
4t^2 & | & \cos(t\pi)\\
8t & | & \frac{\sin(t\pi)}{\pi} & \Rightarrow & 4t^2·\frac{\sin(t\pi)}{\pi}\\
8 & | & \frac{1}{\pi}·\left( \frac{\mbox{-}\cos(t\pi)}{\pi} \right) & \Rightarrow & 
{\color{orange}(-1)}·(8t)·\frac{1}{\pi}\left( \frac{\mbox{-}\cos(t\pi)}{\pi} \right)\\
0 & | & \frac{\mbox{-}1}{(\pi)^2}·\left( \frac{\sin(t\pi)}{\pi} \right) & \Rightarrow &
8·\frac{\mbox{-}1}{(\pi)^2}\left( \frac{\sin(t\pi)}{\pi} \right)\\
\vdots& | & \vdots & \Rightarrow & {{\color{orange}(-1)}·0\dots}
\end{matrix}$$

>- Evaluando en sus limites:
>$$I^{``}_{3}=\left[ \frac{4}{\pi}·t^{2}\sin(t\pi)+
\frac{8}{\pi^{2}}·t\cos(t\pi)-\frac{8}{\pi^{3}}·\sin(t\pi)
\right]\Bigg |^{2}_{0}$$
>
>$$I^{``}_{3}=\left(\frac{4}{\pi}-\frac{8}{\pi^{3}}\right)·{\color{orange}\cancelto{{\color{Orange} 0 }}{{\color{Gray} \sin(t\pi) }}}+\frac{8}{\pi^{2}}·(2{\color{orange}\cancelto{{\color{Orange} 1 }}{{\color{Gray} \cos(2\pi) }}}-0)$$
>
>$$I^{``}_{3}=\frac{16}{\pi^{2}}$$

>Finalmente en la integral principal $I_3$.
>$$I_3=\frac{1}{2}·I^{`}_{3}-I^{``}_{3}+\int \frac{1}{2}·t^{4}+4\,dt=
\frac{I^{`}_{3}}{2}-I^{``}_{3}+\left[
\frac{1}{2}·\frac{t^5}{5}+4t
\right]\Bigg |^{2}_{0}$$
>
>$$I_3=\frac{1}{2}·\left( 
\frac{8}{\pi^{2}}-\frac{3}{\pi^{4}}\right)-
\frac{16}{\pi^{2}}+\left[
\frac{1}{10}·(2^5-0)+4·(2-0)
\right]$$
>
>$$I_3=\frac{1}{\pi^{2}}·(4-16)-\frac{3}{2\pi^{4}}+\left(\frac{16}{5}+8\right)=
\frac{\mbox{-}12}{\pi^{2}}-\frac{3}{2\pi^{4}}+\left(\frac{16+40}{5}\right)$$
>
>$$I_{3}=\frac{56}{5}-\frac{12}{\pi^{2}}-\frac{3}{2\pi^{4}}$$
>
>En la ecuacion.
>$$V_{\varepsilon}=\sqrt{\frac{1}{2}·\left( 
\frac{56}{5}-\frac{12}{\pi^{2}}-\frac{3}{2\pi^{4}}
\right)}=\sqrt{ \frac{28}{5}-\frac{6}{\pi^{2}}-\frac{3}{4\pi^{4}}}\Rightarrow\quad V_{\varepsilon}=2.233$$

>- Para el factor de forma.
>$$f.f.=\frac{2.233}{-2.203}\Rightarrow\quad f.f.=-1.014$$
